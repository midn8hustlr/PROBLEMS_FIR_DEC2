Implementation Hints

1. Use two separate shift registers for polyphase decomposition (one for odd samples, 
   one for even samples). Each needs 8 entries.

2. The symmetric pre-sums should be computed and registered at the right time in the 
   pipeline so they're stable during the two-phase multiplication window.

3. For the newest odd sample, consider whether it's already in the shift register or 
   still on the input when you need to use it.

4. Bitwidths: pre-sum is 9-bit, product is 17-bit, final output is 20-bit.
